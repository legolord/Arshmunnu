The Almanac
The calendar of Arshmunnu
The wheel of the year — sixteen months across four seasons, four hundred and eighty days. Turn the wheel, read any month’s decades, mark festivals and the present day of the Chronicle, reckon a day-of-year to its date, and convert between the reckonings: After Unification, the Foundation of Gothia, the Calendar of the Fall, and the Marian count. Day-notes link straight into the Concordance.

The Concordance
One canon, cross-referenced
The Archive’s shared register of people, houses, gods, places, and events. Each record carries a summary, aliases, and source, and any text across the suite can link to it with [[double brackets]] — with backlinks showing everything that mentions a given name. The spine that turns many tools into one Archive.
