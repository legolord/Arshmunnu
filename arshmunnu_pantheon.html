Divinity of Arshmunnu

The sacred pantheon of the Great and Eternal World — from the Primordial Four who shaped creation to the Nine Seats of Gehennom's infernal depths.

29 Deities · 9 Archdemons · 2 Traditions

I. The Birth of the Aethernum Nexus

In the eternal expanse preceding all existence, the Aethernum Nexus emerged from the primordial Void. This nexus, a wellspring of pure energy, became the core of life, souls, and the very essence of creation. From its luminous essence, four divine beings arose — each embodying a distinct aspect of creation and governance.

II. The Divine Acts of Creation

With their unique powers, the four deities collaboratively forged the world. Elara crafted the Latentium — a transparent dome enveloping the world, an unbreachable barrier against the Void. Amen illuminated the celestial sphere with stars, each serving as a steadfast sentinel to uphold the balance between creation and oblivion. Achmin ignited the Sun and Moon, their radiant light acting as perpetual guardians. Kohe'Samel established the Moon's luminescence and imparted the profound secrets of the Aethernum Nexus to all beings.

Together, they summoned forth diverse races: Elara nurtured elves and fey. Achmin created beasts and the beastfolk of Luminaria. Yer'shalem fashioned Chamalians and majestic dragons. Amen later created Angels and Prellings.

III. The Rise of Amen's Ambition

As creation flourished, Amen's ambition grew insatiable. Seeking to consolidate power and assert dominion over all creation, he embarked on a campaign to subdue his divine counterparts. Achmin was slain, his celestial dominion usurped. Kohe'Samel's memory was erased, bending Yer'shalem into servitude. Elara was shattered into four fragments, scattered across different continents to weaken her influence. One of the new Elarias — an alter ego embodying chaos and darkness — challenged Amen, but was ultimately slain by Kohe'Samel/Yer'shalem.

"For Amen desired not only order, but to be the only order. Thus rose the Revolt of Amen, wherein Achmin was slain, Kohe'Shamel was bound, and Elaria was shattered. From that violence came the world's great wound: the Crack, and all the dread that breathes from it."

IV. The Ascendance of Yer'shalem

After enduring Amen's tyranny, Yer'shalem rose as the leading god. Harnessing the remaining strength of the Aethernum Nexus and fuelled by the desire to restore balance, Yer'shalem confronted Amen. With wisdom and might, Yer'shalem killed Amen, ending his reign of domination and restoring equilibrium to the cosmos. Thus Yer'shalem ascended as the supreme god, overseeing the harmonious balance of creation. The Aethernum Nexus remains the lifeblood of existence; the scattered shards of Elara hint at the ever-present potential for both creation and chaos.

V. The Two Traditions

Two great traditions shape how Arshmunnu understands the divine. The Ethernexa's Myth holds Amen as the primordial entity — the Composer Everlasting — with Astr'y'lis and Patterran as companions, stars hung as sentinels. The Imperial Myth sees the Aethernum Nexus as the primordial core, with Achmin, Amen, Kohe'Samel, and Elara arising from it. Later ages would raise new names to heaven and set crowns upon the unseen — but the elder songs keep their calm refrain: "the world standeth because Amen measureth, because Yer'shalem buildeth, and because the wild remembers what men forget."

The Nine Seats of Gehennom

Bound within the infernal layers of the Nine Hells, the Nine Seats of Gehennom are each ruled by an archdemon personifying a specific sin or principle of evil. Though they feud constantly, the archdemons recognise the need to maintain a delicate balance — an all-out war among themselves would threaten Gehennom's stability and invite the wrath of greater cosmic powers.

Alliances & Family Bonds

Makten & Loll → Niobe
The Archangel of Power and God of Trickery are the adoptive parents of Niobe, the Goddess of Law — a paradoxical lineage that shaped her absolute lawful nature.
Noxus & Lucemir/Urie
The Gods of Darkness and Light are counterparts — Noxus's chaotic good nature and Lucemir's radiant heroism make them rivals philosophically but not enemies.
Yer'shalem & Silman
Yer'shalem is the near face of order; Silman the far face of wild balance. They are not foes, but their worship clashes — the Hand for the house, the Warden for the wood.
Ian & Reor
The God of Creation and God of Destruction are said to be husband and wife — rivals-to-lovers, their eternal dance the heartbeat of the world's cycle.
Lucemir & Clara
The God of Light and the Goddess of Artefacts share a close bond — Clara was a blade-maiden before her ascension, and Lucemir her companion in the mortal realm.

Domains in Conflict

Fea vs. Kohe'Samel
Fea, Goddess of Good Knowledge, represents wisdom for the benefit of all. Kohe'Samel, the Fallen God of Communication, twists knowledge into secrecy and chaos — their domains are mirror images.
Kerim & Mirr
Both govern conflict and dominance — Kerim in mortal warfare, Mirr in draconic hegemony. Their worshippers occasionally clash when armies meet dragon-warlords.
Arod & Demes
Death and birth exist in perfect tension — Arod the Goddess of Death and Demes the Goddess of Agriculture and Birth represent the eternal cycle, their domains inseparable.
Ya'in & Pix
Ya'in's structured, responsible approach to magic contrasts sharply with Pix's capricious, Neutral Evil fey magic — both govern spellcraft, but for wholly different ends.
Eiccalh & All
The God of Corruption is the universal enemy — his whispers seek to unravel every institution, every faith, every alliance. Every other deity's domain is threatened by his decay.

Mortal Cult Conflicts

Imperial vs. Ethernexa
The Imperial cult deifies Achilleus Chliarinos himself as a god — a figure absent from all pre-imperial religion. The Ethernexa tradition rejects this entirely.
Escapian Tradition
Escapia strongly rejects the worship of Amen as the "Eternal Composer," blaming him for cosmic tyranny. They honour Kohe'Shamel, Achmin (as the Dead God), and ancestral spirits.
Divinity of Elaria
Elara's scattered fragment-worshippers in some regions are unaware of the great shattering — they worship her as an unbroken, ever-present Mother of Nature, calling her the Divinity of Elaria.
