Codex of War

The laws that govern the battlefield

Victory Conditions

  • Occupation: Control the enemy Command Seat for 6 consecutive turns.
  • Annihilation: Slay the enemy Commander (♚).
  • Scouts cannot hold a Command Seat.

The Board

  • 10 × 8 squares. Imperial Legion starts at the bottom, Free Coalition at the top.
  • Center Zone: The four central squares are the contested heart of the battlefield.
  • Walls: Engineers may construct walls that block movement and line of sight. Walls crumble after 8 turns.

The Pieces

♚
Commander
The soul of the army

Moves one square in any direction, or leaps two squares (ignores the intervening square). If he falls, the battle is lost. Rally the Line (Imperial only): once per game, order every Imperial Spearman to advance one square — each steps forward only if the square ahead is open, otherwise it holds. Any Spearman reaching the enemy rank is promoted.

♝
Lieutenant (Coalition)
The flanker

Moves one square in any direction, or slides two squares diagonally over empty ground. Special: swap places with any allied piece standing on a same-colored square anywhere on the board.

♗
Bishop (Imperial)
The standard-bearer

The standard-bearer of the Legion. Moves one square in any direction, or slides two squares diagonally over empty ground. Conversion: in place of capturing, turn an adjacent enemy Spearman to the Imperial cause — it changes sides where it stands and fights for the Legion.

♞
Cavalry
Thundering hooves

Moves in an L-shape like a chess knight — 2 squares in one direction then 1 square perpendicular, leaping over pieces. Walls block cavalry leaps — cannot jump over or land on a wall. Also may capture adjacent enemies without moving onto their square.

🏹
Archer
Death from afar

Moves one square in any direction. Ranged attack: Shoots 2 or 3 squares in cardinal directions (N, S, E, W), passing over any pieces. Cannot shoot through walls or shield walls. Two adjacent Spearmen form a shield wall.

♟
Spearman
The shield wall

Moves one square forward, or two squares from its starting rank (the path must be clear); captures diagonally forward. Promotes to Officer upon reaching the enemy back rank. Shield Wall: Two orthogonally adjacent Spearmen of the same team form a shield wall — enemy Archers cannot target them.

👁
Scout
The ghost

Moves two squares in any direction, leaping the intervening square. Evasion: When captured, instead of dying, the Scout instantly retreats to any open square on your back rank. Can only evade once.

⚒
Engineer
Fortifier

Moves one square in any direction. Special: Build a Wall on any adjacent empty square (not a Command Seat). Walls are impassable terrain that block movement and line of sight and crumble after 8 full turns. Only a Catapult can destroy a wall early.

♜
Officer
Field command

Promoted Spearman. Moves one square in any direction. Command Aura: After moving, you may select one adjacent ally (except another Officer) and immediately take a free move with that piece.

🔯
Mage
Arcane artillery

Moves one square in any direction. Special: push an enemy within a diamond — two squares orthogonally (N/S/E/W) or one square in any of the eight directions. The target is shoved one square away; if blocked, it is expelled from the battlefield.

♘
Knight (Imperial)
The vanguard

Moves or captures one square orthogonally (no diagonals), or two squares straight toward the enemy line over clear ground — and it may strike a foe at that two-square range, not just adjacent ones. Twin Strike: press the ability button to spend a charge (once every 5 full rounds) and take two moves in one turn.

♖
Catapult
Siege engine

Moves one square in any direction to reposition, but cannot capture an adjacent enemy. Bombardment (cardinal lines, lobbing over intervening pieces): destroys an enemy standing exactly three squares away, and shatters a wall two or three squares away. It is the only piece that can break a wall.

🪄
Arod's Servant (Coalition)
The conjurer

Moves one square in any direction. Conjure: once every 5 full rounds, summon a fresh Spearman onto an adjacent empty square — a slow but endless wellspring of levies.

Additional Laws

  • Turn Order: Imperial Legion moves first, then Free Coalition.
  • Battlefield: The field is a 10×10 grid; each side fields 20 warriors.
  • Imperial Bishop: The Bishop slides two squares diagonally and, in place of capturing, may convert an adjacent enemy Spearman to the Imperial cause — the spearman changes sides where it stands.
  • Imperial Rally: Once per game, the Imperial Commander orders every Spearman to advance one square; each moves only if the square ahead is open.
  • Knight's Reach: The Imperial Knight may capture a foe two squares straight ahead, the same as its advance — not only adjacent enemies.
  • Knight's Twin Strike: Activate from the ability button to move the Imperial Knight twice in one turn — usable once every 5 full rounds.
  • Arod's Conjuring: The Coalition Servant summons a Spearman every 5 full rounds.
  • Walls: Impassable terrain that decays after 8 turns. Only a Catapult can break one early; no piece may pass through or capture a wall.
  • Seat Occupation: Only non-Scout pieces can hold a Command Seat.

Chronicles of Arshmunnu

The history and tradition of Strategium

Origins

Strategium is the traditional war game of the As'hikan Nobility, played for centuries in the grand halls of the Imperial College of Warfare and the war tents of the Free Coalition alike. It is said that no officer of the As'hikan legions may command on a true battlefield until he has first proven his mind upon the Strategium board.

"This game is taught to every As'hikan officer before he sees a real battlefield."
— Excerpt from the Manual of Arms, Imperial College of Warfare, Year 108 AU

The Two Powers

The Imperial Legions

Representing the might of the As'hikan Empire, the Imperial Legions fight beneath the golden banner of the seated emperor. Their war tent — the Command Seat at E1 — is a symbol of absolute authority. The Legions favor disciplined ranks, shield walls, and the thunderous charge of their cavalry.

The Free Coalition

A confederation of free cities, mountain clans, and disaffected nobles who stand against imperial dominion. Their Command Seat at E10 is the battle drum around which their scattered forces rally. The Coalition relies on mobility, ambush, and the arcane arts of their Magi.

"The board is the battlefield, and the mind is the sword."
