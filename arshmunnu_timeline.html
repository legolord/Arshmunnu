Ancient Times · Mythic Age · Before Time
Before the First Dawn
The Age of the Four Gods · The Pre-Human Epoch · The Era of Creation
∞ BU — Beyond Recorded Memory
Before humans trod the earth, before kingdoms or chronicles, the gods made the world from the Void. Chamalians, Centaurs, Dragons, Angels, and the Fey walked a world where divinity was not myth but presence. The Grand Deluge sealed one age and opened all others.

Founding Events
∞
Birth of the Aethernum Nexus
From the primordial Void emerged the Nexus — a wellspring of pure energy, the core of life, souls, and the very essence of creation. The four great gods arose from its luminous essence.
∞
The Four Divine Beings Emerge
Achmin (the Luminary Sovereign) ignited the Sun and Moon. Amen (the Eternal Composer) wove the stars as the Pillars of Latentium. Kohe'Samel/Yer'shalem (the Harvested Sage) taught all beings to harness the Nexus. Elara (the Guardian of Flora) crafted the Latentium dome, raising a barrier against the Void.
∞
The Genesis of Races
Elara breathed into life Elves and Fey. Achmin shaped Beastfolk. Yer'shalem fashioned Chamalians (lizardfolk) and Dragons. Amen created Angels and Prellings. The world became a living tapestry of divine intention.
∞
The First Sin — Amen's Revolt
Amen the Eternal Composer, seizing ambition, slew the other gods and established tyranny over creation. The rebellious angel Sin led the uprising against Amen. The War of the Four Gods tore the world's harmony apart, thinning the Latentium dome and letting the Void seep through.
Deep Antiquity
The Grand Deluge & The Crack
Amen's revolt tore the earth and opened the Crack — a wound where the Void's breath touched the world. To seal it, the elder Chamalians wrought a terrible remedy, and the world answered with catastrophic waters. The Grand Deluge drowned the northern lands, leaving only the highest peaks as islands. Escapia, the northern continent, was severed from Exelia.
Pre-Human
The Pre-Human Ages of Dominion
In the centuries before humanity's rise, Chamalians dominated Exelia with theocratic order. Centauric dynasties controlled the mountain passes and plains. Dragons were sovereign over the skies. Beastfolk of Luminaria thrived in the south. This era ended when Yer'shalem slew Amen and established new divine order — but the old races began to decline.
Pre-Human
The First Holy 16 Humans
Kohe'Shamel's long work returned in secret — mankind came late to the world, after the drowned centuries. The First Holy 16 Humans founded the first human tribes, beginning the long arc of human civilisation and dominance that would eventually reshape all of Arshmunnu.
Early Antiquity
The Genocides — Centaurs and Chamalians
As human kingdoms grew bold, the ancient non-human peoples were violently displaced. The Genocide of the Centaurs erased the great centauric dynasties. The Genocide of the Chamalians — recorded in the Fabulae and the Impernexa — drove lizardfolk north to Escapia, where their isolated communities would survive for millennia, untouched by Exelian politics.

First Age · Age of Heroes and Legend
The Age of Heroes and Legend
The Imvien Age · Age of the Great Kingdoms · The Era of the Remaining Flame
Deep Antiquity — c. 715 BU
The great Imvien Kingdom shines as its brightest. Caldère stands as the world's beacon of peace. Marian the Firm unifies Armorica. The Confederation of the Remaining Flame is born from grief, the Five Towers of Magic are raised, and the Coalition Wars begin to tear the old world apart.

Key Events
~2000 BU
House Charan Founded
Octavia the Melodist and Recentibus the Wise establish the ancient Armorican royal line — its motto "Through Song and Thought, We Reign" — laying the philosophical and musical foundations of Armorican culture.
Antiquity
The Imvien Kingdom at Its Height
Elderglen — "a city of pearl towers and silver-roofed colleges, called the Breath of Hymnia" — stands as the Imvien capital. Caldère serves as a sanctuary of scholars, healers, and peacekeepers. No army stands within its walls. It needs none. The Five Songs of the Confederation are composed.
Antiquity
The Five Towers of Magic Established
The great Arcane Towers are raised across Exelia — Red, Blue, Purple, White, and Green — each holding its own sovereign magic tradition. The Arcane Council of Exelia (Council of the Fivefold Accord) is formed as a binding arbitration body. They would only intervene six times in the centuries to follow; each time it changed the world.
~435 BU
Marian the Firm Begins the Unification of Armorica
A warrior-queen rises in Helen, conquering the Two Duchies of Kovesk and Greenwood, seizing Nerves, subduing Somelsk. She crowns herself on the Spire of Helen without priest or song, placing the Forge-Crown (eight nails, one from each province) upon her own head. Her Oath: "No border shall divide my people that cannot be crossed by bread, and guarded by law."
~400 BU
The Szewo-Armorican War & The Grand Immigration
Marian invades Szewia seeking fertile plains. Ten years of bitter war. Queen Seraphin of Barcazsi is slain by her own brother — then returns from death as something between living and dead. She leads the Grand Immigration: all Szewians march in silence into the Endless Desert. Marian stands before empty Barcazsi: "This was not victory. This was the ending of a people."
~380 BU
The Fall of Caldère — The Light Goes Out
Armorican warlords and Szewian high-princes form a pact: Caldère shall fall, its vaults split, its scholars spared if they yield. They attack from two sides. The last five peacekeepers of Caldère set themselves ablaze on the central stair rather than serve either army. The beacon of peace becomes a pyre. Imvien scribes will never write its name again — they call it only "The Light Unseen."
~360 BU
Confederation of the Remaining Flame
From the collapse of the Imvien Kingdom, five island-realms form a confederation bound not by crown but by memory — Avilon, Aridia, Abilia, As'hika, and Alodia. Their oath: "This land was not won, it was kept." Karun's silent crown would later make six. The Confederation would endure until the Armorican advance sundered the kindled isles.
~715 BU
The Coalition Wars Begin
A series of devastating conflicts shatter the old world order. Kingdoms that once traded in good faith take up arms in an era of shifting alliances, betrayals, and total war. The old Imvien peace is definitively gone. This marks the end of the First Age and the beginning of the Age of Chaos.

Second Age · 715 BU – 0 AU
The Age of Chaos
The Era of Coalition Wars · Age of Fire and Ruin · The Revolutionary Age
715 BU — 0 AU
The Coalition Wars shatter every old order. Kingdoms bleed into one another; warlords seize what gods and law cannot protect. From the ruin of the old world, new powers rise — and from the crucible of revolutionary warfare, one commoner forges an empire that will define the next two thousand years.

Key Events
715 BU
The Coalition Wars Erupt
The old world order shatters. A series of devastating conflicts across Exelia destroys the last remnants of the Imvien-era peace. Kingdoms that had cooperated for centuries find themselves in total war. Power shifts constantly; no single force can hold the continent. The Arcane Council is drawn into political conflict as the Five Towers take sides.
Mid-BU
The Tower War & The Fall of the Arcane Council
The Five Towers — Red (Ruden/Armorica), Blue (Alodia), Purple (Polia), White (Chamalian Papacy), Green (Tree Island) — take sides in the political conflicts. The Council of Five Towers meets one final time in a fractured projection. The White Tower invokes Law; the Blue claims neutrality died with the centaurs; the Purple laughs and says "Let the book burn faster this time." The Green says nothing. Then the projections snap. Boque Tower falls. "Five stand where one fell. Let none fall alone."
~50 BU
Achilleus I Chliarinos Born
A man of common birth in Alodia. He flees the Alodian civil war to As'hika, where the embers of the old Confederation still glow. His tactical genius, leadership, and mythic presence will eventually draw loyalty from every tribe, city, and broken kingdom across Exelia. His rise is not yet — but the age is being prepared for him.
~5 BU
The Revolutionary Wars Begin
New ideas challenge the old established powers — that greatness could rise from anywhere, that kingdoms could be forged by merit rather than blood. Achilleus channels the revolutionary spirit into a military campaign that becomes the engine of a new era. The revolutionary slogan "From Ashes, Empire" is born in practice before it is ever written.
0 AU
The Revolutionary Wars End — The Empire Declared
Achilleus I Chliarinos declares the As'hika Empire at Year 0 AU, unifying Armorica, Alodia, Szewia, Chamalia, and Gothia under one banner through a blend of conquest, diplomacy, and mythic authority. The Second Age ends. A new era — one that will last two thousand years — begins.

Third Age · 0 AU – 2078 AU
The Imperial Age
The As'hikan Age · The Pax As'hikana · The Age of Empire
0 AU — 2078 AU · ~2,078 years
The As'hikan Empire rises and dominates Exelia for two thousand years. Its Pax As'hikana — the golden age of law, roads, orders, and cultural flourishing — lasts six and a half centuries. Then comes the long dying: the War of Two Houses, the Red Ash Plague, the Age of Pretenders, and the final slow collapse.
▶ Reveal the Chronicle
Key Events
0 AU
The Empire Declared — Year Zero
Achilleus I Chliarinos establishes the As'hika Empire. A new calendar begins. The commoner dynasty House Chliarinos founds what will be the world's defining political institution. Marriage to Isabelle IX of Armorica (House Charan) provides noble legitimacy. The Phoenix Agency is founded as the empire's secret intelligence service.
21 AU
Achilleus Withdraws — Death in the Desert
Weary of rule, Achilleus abandons the palace without ceremony, riding east in a soldier's cloak. He wanders the Karun Desert — where he fought his greatest wars — for three weeks, speaking to the ghosts of the fallen. He does not rise on the twenty-first day. His legacy: "One law, one land, one life — all shall kneel, not in chains, but in hope."
43 AU
The Pax As'hikana Begins
Under Philippe James the Unifier, the institutional empire begins. One law, one language, one custom across all provinces. The great Viae Imperiales road network is built. The Church of Unity is formalised. The Imperial Circle, Golden Rose, and Phoenix Agency are established as the pillars of imperial stability. Six centuries of peace and cultural flourishing begin.
213 AU
The Great Census of Margarethe I
The first imperial census counts souls, hearths, beasts, granaries, and guild rolls across all provinces. Margarethe I — "Mother of the Empire" — also extends the Viae Imperiales to the Bayner Grand Lake. Her death was mourned for ten days across all capitals.
403–459 AU
Elionor I the Veiled — The Spymistress Emperor
The former head of the Phoenix Agency ascends the throne. She strengthened the empire's grip on magic, guilds, and the high courts. Her court ran with unsettling efficiency. The most feared of the peaceful emperors.
646–705 AU
Julian II the Long-Reigning
The wisest emperor — 59 years, the longest reign in imperial history. A master of diplomacy and restraint who kept rebellious nobles in line through respect, not terror. He accidentally married his gardener Eleanor, was perpetually out-justed by Marcus of House Morion, and received the legendary Sword of Achilleus I as a gift. His legacy quote: "An emperor is not flame, but hearth. He does not burn the house — he keeps it warm."
705 AU
Death of Julian II — The Pax Ends
Julian II dies without a clear heir — his sons dead, daughters barred, council split. The succession vacuum explodes into the War of Two Houses (705–743 AU). Two rival Chliarinos branches fight for the throne for 38 years. This sets the precedent for the Late Empire: private armies, plagues, fragmented authority, and noble kings in all but name.
777–781 AU
The Red Ash Plague
A devastating epidemic kills millions and exposes the empire's inability to coordinate a response to crisis. The public wells and hospitals of the Pax had decayed or been seized by private interests. The Graying Fever, the White Forest Collapse in Avilon, and other arcane disasters compound the devastation.
793–817 AU
Achilleus IV — The Period of Pause
The last truly effective emperor. He crushed five rebellions, restored Lundenford, and reconvened the Imperial Council. Harsh in method, just in purpose — "If I must be iron, let it be for the sake of peace." His death in 817 AU began the true final decline. No worthy heir rose. The empire continued its death as a corpse dressed in imperial robes.
821 AU
The Szewian-Chamalian Concord
The eastern Pontifex declares Julian II's successors "unholy pretenders" — the empire is spiritually dead in the eyes of the Chamalian Church. Chamalian monasteries become rallying points for separatists, rebels, and new mystic orders. The empire continues in name; in reality, it is already hollow.
~1960 AU
The Empire Collapses into the Era of Division
The weight of its vast reach, its hollow bureaucracy, its plagues and rebellions, finally breaks the last sinew of imperial authority. The world fractures into numerous small states and kingdoms. The Fourth Era begins — the Era of Division.
2078 AU
House Chliarinos Loses the Throne
The dynasty that forged an empire from common birth loses its last claim to the throne. The name Chliarinos carries immense symbolic weight but no political power. The Phoenix Agency, now without a master, acts independently — seeking restoration through any means necessary. Prince Aurelian Chliarinos (c. 2125 AU) rises as a claimant to the legacy.

Fourth Age · ~2078 AU – ~2600 AU
The Temple's Age
Early Modern Age · Age of Exploration · The Era of the Chamalian Papacy · The War of Two Continents
~2078 AU — ~2600 AU
Into the vacuum left by the fallen empire, the Chamalian Papacy seizes dominion. Temples replace fortresses; inquisitors replace soldiers. But the south stirs — the beastmen of Luminaria resist; As'try'lys expands under the God of the Sun; the Moon Goddess is absent; and Exelian explorers begin pushing beyond the known world.

Key Events
~2078 AU
The Chamalian Papacy Seizes the Vacuum
After the As'hikan Empire's collapse, the Chamalian Papacy imposes its rigid doctrine across Exelia. Temples replace fortresses. Inquisitors replace soldiers. Heresy becomes the crime of the age. The Church presents itself as the ultimate authority over both spiritual and temporal affairs, erasing imperial tradition and alternative beliefs wherever it can.
~2100 AU
As'try'lys — Rise of the Sun God's Kingdom
In the south, As'try'lys — a dominant, expansionist kingdom ruled under the God of the Sun — rises as the military counterweight to the Papacy. Its strength is unmatched but its people are strained by war. The southern beastmen of Luminaria find themselves caught between two imperial ambitions.
~2100–2300 AU
The War of Two Continents & The Beastmen's Resistance
The War on Abila Island becomes a microcosm of the age — a struggle for identity and sovereignty against both Exelia and As'try'lys. Tribes of lionfolk, serpentfolk, and avian beastmen cooperate for the first time, their unity forged in the crucible of survival. The Selunarian Remnants search for their goddess and the Staff of the Moon.
~2125 AU
Prince Aurelian Chliarinos — Imperial Claimant
A self-proclaimed heir to the As'hikan legacy, Aurelian seeks to revive the empire against the Chamalian Papacy's dominion. His claim is hotly debated — many see him as a relic, others as a beacon of hope. The Phoenix Agency, still operating in shadow, provides covert support to factions that seek to preserve the imperial memory.
~2200 AU
Chamalian Underground — Old Rites, Desert Prophets, Jasmine Revival
Underground sects flourish across the Chamalian lands: the Old Rites, the Desert Prophets, and the Jasmine Revival in the southern mountains. The Church splits: some clergy loyal to the throne, others dreaming of the Restoration. The great shrines become tourist relics; Chamalian identity remains proud but dormant.
~2300 AU
Age of Exploration Begins
Exelian explorers begin venturing beyond familiar shores, driven by the fragmented post-imperial world's desperate need for resources, routes, and advantage. Legends of the northern continent Escapia — drowned in the Grand Deluge myth — begin to be taken seriously again. The first expeditions push north through fog and forgotten waters.

Fifth Age · ~2600 AU – 3051 AU
The Age of Revolutions
The Era of Republican Uprisings · The Tower War's Legacy · The Birth of the SSC
~2600 AU — 3051 AU
The old monarchies crack. The Armorican Revolution — sparked at a wedding — spreads revolutionary fervour across the world. Wars of Coalition pit monarchists against republicans. New republics rise from the fires of old crowns. The Great War of Cult reshapes the world once more, and the age closes in catastrophe and transformation.
▶ Reveal the Chronicle
Key Events
~2600 AU
The Armorican Revolution
It begins at Isabelle's wedding to Thomas Kovesd. The king and queen are slain. Revolutionary fervour spreads across the world almost overnight — the idea that monarchy is not a divine right but a social contract, breakable by the people, takes root in every city square. Alodia swings republican. Frontian radicals take to the streets.
~2620 AU
The Wars of Coalition — Monarchists vs. Revolutionaries
Monarchists (Gothia, Chamalia, Szewia, the monarchic factions of Armorica and Alodia) clash with the revolutionary coalition (Alodian Republic, Frontian radicals). The wars drag across the continent, redrawing borders, breaking aristocratic families, and forging new political realities. The Red Tower of Ruden backs the old order; the Blue Tower of Alodia backs the revolution.
~2700 AU
Rise of Republican Ideology — Birth of Communalism
Philosopher Rael Eramenth, writing in Alodia during the Fourth Era, becomes the foundational theorist of Communalism — Council Communalism, Flame Syndicalism, Ancestral Communalism, and eventually the Post-Leniskian Neo-Councilism that will birth the SSC. His ideas spread through clandestine networks: coded tattoos, ephemeral ink, the Silent Drop underground.
~2800 AU
Alodian Civil War — The As'hikan Civil War
Multiple factions contest control — the Avilon Confederation, former republicans, monarchist remnants. The civil war reshapes Alodia and As'hika's political landscape. The Phoenix Agency, now rogue, takes sides in secret. The old imperial structures are finally, definitively dismantled — or buried so deep they become myth.
~3000 AU
The Great War of Cult
A catastrophic, world-reshaping conflict that closes the Fourth Era and marks the end of the Age of Revolutions. Cult movements, theological conflicts, and the last convulsive death-throes of the old order converge in a war that reshapes the political map of Exelia entirely. The SSC's predecessor movements rise directly from its ashes.
3051 AU
End of the Fourth Era — A World Remade
The catastrophic events of the Great War of Cult close the Fourth Era and open the Fifth. The world emerges changed beyond recognition — old certainties gone, new powers rising from the ashes. The three great superpowers (Alodia, Armorican Crown, SSC) begin to consolidate their positions for the century ahead.

Sixth Age · 3051 AU – ~4069 AU
The Age of Steam and Magic
The Modern Age · The Age of the Cold War · The Fifth Era · Wilhelm's Time
3051 AU — ~4069 AU
The three superpowers — the Republic of Alodia, the Armorican Crown, and the Soviet Socialist Commonwealth — dominate a world rebuilding from revolutionary ash. Steam-and-magic engineering transforms daily life. A cold ideological war runs beneath every diplomatic exchange. The beastmen kingdoms rise. And one figure — Wilhelm — changes everything.
▶ Reveal the Chronicle
Key Events
3051 AU
The Fifth Era Begins — A World in Recovery
The Great War of Cult reshapes the world and societies begin to recover. New advancements in governance, craftsmanship, and exploration. Old ways reconsidered. The shadow of the past lingers — but for the first time in centuries, there is cautious forward movement.
~2295 AU
The Three Superpowers Consolidate (c. 2295 AU in-world)
The Republic of Alodia (Jaenobiet Parliamentary Republic — naval and trade power, capital Imvien Bay, leader President Amadis Vernel). The Armorican Crown (Constitutional Monarchy — elite military and church networks, capital Elderglen, Queen Isabelle XII). The Soviet Socialist Commonwealth (Tri-Republic Federated Communalism — industrial self-sufficiency, capitals C'aras Irus / Vranahall / Redfort, Presidium: Zumindest, Anastasia, Zovak). A cold war of ideology, proxy conflicts, and diplomatic brinkmanship defines the age.
~3100–3500 AU
The Age of Steam-and-Magic Engineering
Mages and scholars integrate magic with practical innovations, leading to new technologies that blur the line between the arcane and the mechanical. Lundenford Academy becomes the hub of magical engineering. Firearms appear (Brassbolt revolvers, clockwork mechanisms, gyro-stabilised rifles). Steam-powered constructs and clockwork drakes enter military use. The world accelerates.
~3500 AU
Rise of the Beastmen Kingdoms
In the southern lands of Luminaria, the beastmen rebuild after centuries of strife. Selunarian exiles dream of their goddess's return in the perpetual winter of Seluna. In As'try'lys, the dominance of the God of the Sun weakens as beastman warlords carve out territories. Interactions between Exelia and Luminaria become increasingly significant — and tense.
~3800 AU
The Fourth Millennium — Wilhelm's Time
The Armorican Kingdom under a weakened crown struggles to maintain control over the Two Duchies (Greenwood and Kovesd). Magic, long suppressed or forgotten, resurges. New schools blend traditional practices with rediscovered knowledge. Wilhelm — born into a marquess family of the Two Duchies — becomes the central figure of a transformative era whose actions ripple outward into every corner of the world.
~4000 AU
The Chamalian Papacy's Final Decline
The once-mighty Papacy has decayed to a shadow — clinging to a few strongholds, its centralised control fractured by centuries of resistance and internal decay. It reinvents itself as a force of expansion and moral authority, aiming at Escapia as a new mission. But its days of real dominion are over.
~4050 AU
First Contact with Escapia's Shores
Exelian explorers arrive on the shores of Escapia — the northern continent drowned in myth since the Grand Deluge. They find forgotten Chamalian lizardfolk cities and relics. Tensions escalate immediately as the modern Papacy (now led by humans) seeks to claim Escapia, erasing the memory of the lizardfolk genocide from its own doctrine. Escapia becomes the next great flashpoint.

Seventh Age · 4069 AU — Present In Progress
The Age of Information
Age of Diesel and Magic · Age of Cyber, Light and Magic · The Unwritten Age
4069 AU — ??? AU · The Present Unfolding
The world hums with diesel engines and arcane networks. Magic and machine have grown inseparable. Exelians confront the Escapian lizardfolk, their own violent history, and the ambitions of three superpowers in an age of information, proxy wars, and moral reckoning. The ink is not yet dry — the chronicle writes itself in real time.

Known Events — The Unfolding Record
4069 AU
After Wilhelm — The Fifth Era Fully Begins
Wilhelm's transformative actions usher in a period of exploration, rediscovery, and geopolitical upheaval. Exelia rebuilds from its fractured state, inhabitants turning their gaze outward. The Phoenix Agency — weakened but alive — supports factions that aim to preserve Escapia's history and prevent its exploitation.
4069+ AU
The Escapia Confrontation
Exelian explorers arrive on Escapia's shores in numbers, uncovering forgotten Chamalian relics and cities. The modern Chamalian Papacy (now human-led) seeks to claim Escapia as a holy reclamation, erasing the lizardfolk genocide from its doctrine. This sparks ethical debates across Exelia about accountability, reparations, and the morality of conquest. Evidence unearthed in Escapia challenges official narratives.
4069+ AU
Diesel and Magic — The Technological Integration
Diesel engines and arcane networks interweave into the fabric of daily life. Lundenford Academy sends expeditions to Escapia to recover ancient Chamalian relics. Magical engineering blurs the line between the arcane and mechanical further still. Traditionalists resist; progressives push for acceleration. The world moves faster than any chronicle can easily capture.
4069+ AU
The Three-Superpower Cold War Intensifies
Alodia (trade and diplomacy), the Armorican Crown (noble and clergy networks), and the SSC (ideological industrial power) continue their ideological cold war across proxy conflicts in Escapia, the Beastmen territories, and the former Chamalian heartlands. Espionage, broadcast magic, and information networks become the new battlefield.
Near Future
The Age of Cyber — Light and Magic Converge
The horizon of the Seventh Age promises the full integration of arcane networks with information technology — a world where magical computation, enchanted broadcasting, and cyber-arcane infrastructure reshape society as fundamentally as the printing press once did. This age is still being written. The scribes cannot keep pace.

The Next Chronicle Awaits
What ends this age? An Eighth Age waits beyond the horizon — its shape unknown, its name uncoined. The world of Arshmunnu has always moved from order to chaos to order again. Whatever comes next, the Aethernum Nexus still hums beneath the earth, the stars still stand as columns between Latentium and the outer Void, and the ink of memory flows like the rivers of Ruden.
