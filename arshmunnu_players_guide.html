Introduction

Arshmunnu is a late Bronze Age to early Iron Age setting — a world of empires and wilderness, of divine politics and mortal ambition. This guide contains everything you need to play.

What kind of game this is:

  • Danger is earnest. Wounds matter, fear mattereth, and a poor choice may follow thee longer than a scar.
  • Politics is not garnish, but bread. Oaths, seals, reputation, and station are weapons as sharp as steel.
  • Mythic weirdness is real, but not tame. Wonders exist, yet they come with price, attention, and aftermath.
  • Victory is broader than slaughter. Many triumphs are roads opened, trusts earned, and foes not made.

Training Caps

Defence Training caps at Skilled +5. All other trainings can reach Expert +7. Your Calling and technique paths determine which trainings you can advance most effectively.

Combat Basics

Combat Procedure

  1. Establish the scene — combatants, terrain, hazards, objectives
  2. Surprise — if fitting, failed notice means a weak first turn
  3. Initiative — d20 + Agility + training; break ties cleanly
  4. Run turns by AP — keep it brisk

Defence Level (DL)

DL is the target number to hit you. It replaces Armour Class from other systems.

Base DL = 10 + Agility modifier + Fortitude modifier + shield bonus + armour bonus

Both Agility and Fortitude contribute to your DL — being nimble AND tough makes you harder to strike effectively.

To-Hit Roll

d20 + attribute modifier + training bonus vs target's DL

Multi-Strike Penalty

Each additional strike on your turn suffers a cumulative -3 penalty. This prevents "blender turns" and makes tactical choices matter.

Guard Action

Spending 1 AP to Guard gives +2 DL until your next turn. With a shield, this becomes +3 DL. Defence is an action, not a prayer.

Actions

Movement in Combat

Moving twice is risky. If you Dash (move twice in a turn), you cannot Guard on that turn. This teaches that positioning matters — fights become tactical instead of chaotic.

Damage

Damage = Weapon die + attribute modifier. Two-handed weapons deal more damage but cost more AP for certain manoeuvres.

Dual Wielding

You may wield a light weapon in each hand. When you Strike with your main hand, you may make an additional attack with your off-hand for 1 AP (not 2). The off-hand attack uses the weapon's damage die without adding your attribute modifier.

Shield Rules

Shields provide a bonus to DL when you take the Guard action:

  • Buckler: +1 DL, no movement penalty
  • Standard Shield: +2 DL, -1m movement if not Guarding
  • Tower Shield: +3 DL, -2m movement, cannot Dash while equipped

Multiclassing

Arshmunnu allows free-form character development. When you gain a level, you may choose features from any Class available to you, provided you meet the prerequisites. You are not locked into a single path. Record your choices — your character sheet is a story of your training.

Character Creation

Step-by-Step

  1. Choose Concept — Who is your character? A disgraced knight? A void-touched outcast? A scholar of forbidden lore?
  2. Choose Species — Select from the 15 peoples of Arshmunnu. Apply species attribute modifiers.
  3. Choose Calling — Select your Calling (class) and Technique Path. This determines your EP, AP, MP, and core trainings.
  4. Distribute Attributes — All attributes start at 4. You have 28 points to distribute (max 10, min 4 before species mods). Humans get +2 bonus points.
  5. Assign Trainings — Set your training tiers based on your Calling. Core trainings start at Trained (+3). Defence caps at Skilled (+5).
  6. Choose Equipment — Select a starting kit appropriate to your Calling.
  7. Write Background — Name, origin region, alignment, faith, personal history, and driving goal.

Spellcasting

Spellcasting in Arshmunnu is deliberate and costly. Every spell drains Mana (MAN) from the caster's pool.

Casting a Spell

  1. Declare the spell and its target(s)
  2. Spend the AP cost (typically 2-4 AP)
  3. Pay the Mana cost from your Mana pool
  4. Roll casting check (d20 + MAN modifier + training) vs spell DC if contested
  5. Resolve the effect

Mana & Costs

Mana Pool

Your Mana pool = Base + MAN modifier × 4. Mana regenerates slowly — 1 point per hour of rest, or fully after a Long Rest.

Social Encounters

Social encounters in Arshmunnu are contests of will and consequence, not "talk until bored."

Social Combat Structure

  1. Authority context: Does Standing apply here?
  2. Audience: Witnesses change everything.
  3. Leverage: What can each side offer or threaten?
  4. Exit: Deal, compromise, refusal, humiliation, violence, or delay.

Standing

Your reputation and rank in society. Standing adds to social rolls where authority is recognised. It is earned through deeds, not bought:

  • 0 — Unknown: No special weight
  • 1 — Recognised: Known in your community
  • 2 — Respected: Known across a region
  • 3 — Renowned: Known across a kingdom
  • 4 — Famous: Known across the world

Pressure & Bearing

Social pressure can impose conditions like Frightened or Demoralised without drawing steel. A successful Bearing check (BRG + Persuasion/Intimidation/Deception) can force a target to reconsider their position — or break their nerve entirely.

Bearing is also used for command, leadership, and maintaining composure under pressure. A commander with high Bearing can rally troops, while a diplomat can avert war with the right words.

Resting

Short Rest (1 hour)

Recover EP equal to your Fortitude modifier + level. You may use Hit Dice (1d10 + FOR modifier) to recover additional EP. You can spend up to half your level in Hit Dice per Short Rest.

Long Rest (8 hours)

Fully recover EP and Mana. Remove one condition (Shaken, Exhausted, or Frightened). Recover all Hit Dice. If resting in dangerous conditions, you only recover half EP.

Death & Recovery

Downed State

When EP reaches 0, you are Downed. You cannot take normal actions and immediately suffer one Wound.

Death Save

At the end of each round while Downed:

  • Roll Fortitude save
  • Modifiers: -1 per existing Wound, -1 if bleeding/poisoned/neglected, +1 with medical aid
  • Success: Stabilise (still unconscious)
  • Partial: No change
  • Failure: Gain one Death Mark
  • Dire Failure: Gain two Death Marks

Death Marks

At 3 Death Marks, the character dies. Death Marks clear only through proper medical care, long rest in safety, or extraordinary means (magic, divine intervention).

Instant Death

Instant death only occurs from:

  • Deliberate killing blow while Downed
  • Catastrophic events (collapse, incineration, void exposure)
  • Player choosing narrative death

I will play boldly, yet bear consequence without bitterness.

I will share the light and let each companion have their scene.

Combatants

Callings (Player's Guide v2)

Beyond the original ten Callings, the present chapter introduces fifteen further Callings drawn directly from the Council of Hands' revised manuscript. They are organised into four families: Martial, Ranged, Social, and Magical. Each gains Level Features at 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18, plus four Specialities chosen as the character grows.

Martial Callings

1) Legionary — Shield and Line

Disciplined frontline, formation fighter, protector. Main Abilities: Might or Agility; Fortitude; Will. Starting trainings: Defence (Trained), Melee (Trained), Armour (Trained), two skills from Command/Athletics/Survival/Intimidation at Trained, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Shield Doctrine. +1 DL on Guard with shield (total +4); 1 AP reaction to impose -2 on an attack against an adjacent ally.
Lv 3 Hold the Line. If you did not move, foes entering reach suffer -2 to hit you until your next turn.
Lv 6 Formation Step. Adjacent shield-bearers may each spend 1 AP for +2 DL until next turn; movement becomes 0.
Lv 9 Relentless Advance. Once per round, ignore the -2 multi-strike penalty on your second strike.
Lv 12 Iron Discipline. On Will Save success, downgrade Frightened/Panicked by one step.
Lv 15 Veteran of Roads. Short Rest restores +10% max EP; auto-remove Tier I Exhaustion with food and water.
Lv 18 Wall of Men. Once per scene declare The Line: allies within 2 paces gain +2 DL vs melee for one round; cannot be forcibly moved unless they fail Might Save.

Specialities: Shield Wall Tactics; Command (Cohorts); Endurance Marching; Spear and Shield.

2) Duelist — Blade and Measure

Precision melee, focused strikes, counters. Main Abilities: Agility (or Might); Wit; Bearing. Starting: Melee (Trained), Defence (Initiate), two skills at Trained, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Measure and Riposte. If a foe misses you by 5+, 1 AP to make a Swift Strike against them.
Lv 3 True Opening. First Focused Attack each round does not impose the -2 DL Overcommitment after.
Lv 6 Disarming Grace. On Great Success with a Focused Strike, choose Disarm without an opposed save (unless target is two sizes larger or braced).
Lv 9 Footwork of the Narrow Way. If you moved only once (1 AP), no -2 DL overextension.
Lv 12 Needle in the Gaps. Focused Attacks ignore +3 DL from armour/guard/cover (instead of +2). Shields still apply unless a tag allows.
Lv 15 Killing Calm. Wounded/Grievously Wounded penalties reduced by 1.
Lv 18 Master of the Bout. Once per scene declare a Duel: 3 rounds of +2 hit and +2 DL vs one foe, but cannot willingly leave their presence.

Specialities: Parrying Blades; Focused Strikes (Limbs); Acrobatics (Tumbling); Bearing (Court Duels).

3) Vanguard — Breaker of Gates

Heavy hitter, shock, forced movement, intimidation by steel. Main Abilities: Might; Fortitude; Will. Starting: Melee (Trained), Armour (Initiate), Defence (Initiate), two skills at Trained, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Crushing Entry. Heavy Blow hits: save vs push/prone at -2.
Lv 3 Break the Shield. Heavy Blow / Shield-Breaker hit on a shield-bearer: their shield check at Disadvantage.
Lv 6 Unstoppable Step. Once per round, ignore forced movement or prone unless Dire Failure.
Lv 9 Shockwave. Heavy Blow Great Success: a second adjacent foe makes Fortitude Save or is Staggered.
Lv 12 Brutal Certainty. Heavy Blows deal +1 flat damage.
Lv 15 Stone-Blooded. Reduce Crushing damage by 1 always (stacks with shield).
Lv 18 Gatebreaker. Once per scene, 2 AP Might check to break a barrier or formation — an ally passes through without consequence.

Specialities: Athletics (Grappling); Intimidation (Battle Roar); Craft (Siege Tools); Heavy Weapons.

Ranged Callings

4) Archer — Bow and Patience

Main Abilities: Agility or Wit; Will. Starting: Ranged (Trained), two skills at Trained.

Lv 1 Steady Breath — Aimed Shot +3 if you did not move. Lv 3 Thread the Gap — Aimed Shot ignores half cover, reduces concealment one step. Lv 6 Suppressing Fire — 2 AP, -2 to hit; on hit, target Suppressed and cannot Focused next turn unless Will Save. Lv 9 Hunter's Mark — once per scene, marked target takes +1 die step on Great Success. Lv 12 Long Sight — Distant range penalty -2. Lv 15 Calm Under Threat — engagement penalty -4 → -2. Lv 18 The Arrow That Ends a Charge — reaction shot at chargers; on hit, Pinned save.

Specialities: Perception (Hidden Threats); Ranged (Aimed Shots); Survival (Tracking); Stealth (Hide-and-Shoot).

5) Sling-Scout — Stone and Street

Main Abilities: Agility; Wit. Starting: Ranged (Trained), Defence (Initiate), two skills at Trained.

Lv 1 Runner's Shot — moved this turn? still Focused ranged, lose only the +2. Lv 3 Crowdwork — Advantage on Stealth/Deception in crowds. Lv 6 Whistling Stones — Great Success may impose Off-Balance even on minimal damage. Lv 9 Split the Band — Volley keeps damage die (still -2 to hit). Lv 12 Streetwise Aim — use Wit for quick-weapon ranged at 2 AP. Lv 15 Always Moving — two moves = -1 DL overextension (not -2). Lv 18 Scout's Escape — 2 AP to dodge and impose Disadvantage on an attack.

Specialities: Stealth (Urban Shadows); Acrobatics (Rooftops); Deception (False Faces); Ranged (Volley).

6) Crossbowman — Bolt and Brutality

Main Abilities: Wit; Agility; Might. Starting: Ranged (Trained), Craft or Investigation (Trained), one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Braced Reload — Reload -1 AP (min 1). Lv 3 Killing Lane — stationary: +2 to hit foes crossing your line. Lv 6 Bolt Through Mail — Piercing ignores +3 DL (not +2). Lv 9 Pinning Shot — Aimed Shot may choose Pin without Great Success. Lv 12 Mechanist's Hand — repair Damaged ranged in Short Rest, no roll. Lv 15 Cold Loader — no Disadvantage from fear/suppression while reloading (unless Panicked). Lv 18 The Siege Bolt — once per scene, 2 AP, -2 to hit, +2 dice damage. Miss by 5+: weapon Damaged.

Social Callings

Social Callings project Pressure (see below). They do not win battles by damage but by morale, authority, and disruption.

7) Envoy — Tongue and Treaty

Main Abilities: Bearing; Wit; Will. Starting: Persuasion (Trained), Command or Deception (Initiate), two skills at Trained.

Lv 1 Letters and Seals — proper papers grant +2 Standing for access. Lv 3 Calm the Blood — 1 AP Bearing roll vs Composure; remove Shaken from ally or downgrade Panicked → Frightened. Lv 6 The Better Offer — choose the shape of a Partial Success compromise. Lv 9 Public Weight — Great Success shifts Reputation by an additional +1. Lv 12 The Unbroken Face — ignore the first -2 from multiple social actions per round. Lv 15 Pact-Keeper — add Reputation as well as Standing on public Oaths. Lv 18 The Treaty That Stops a War — once per arc, force a parley.

Specialities: Persuasion (Treaties); Lore (Law); Command (Council Procedure); Deception (Polite Lies).

8) Magistrate — Law and Consequence

Main Abilities: Wit; Bearing; Will. Starting: Investigation (Trained), Command or Intimidation (Trained), Lore (Law) (Initiate), one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Formal Authority — Standing applies even to hostile rolls under mandate. Lv 3 Read the Lie — once per scene, demand opposed roll; learn whether and what kind of lie. Lv 6 Writ of Seizure — legal acquisition without coin. Lv 9 Interrogator's Stillness — Will instead of Bearing for intimidation Composure. Lv 12 Chain of Custody — secured evidence -2 to discredit. Lv 15 Sentence — break Composure to 0 may impose Disgraced. Lv 18 The Long Arm — call on lesser officials as faction resource.

Specialities: Investigation (Forensics); Command (Guards); Lore (Imperial Law); Intimidation (Cold Authority).

9) Spymaster — Mask and Knife

Main Abilities: Wit; Bearing; Agility; Will. Starting: Deception (Trained), Stealth (Trained), two skills at Initiate.

Lv 1 False Face — cover identity passes casual scrutiny; formal opposed with +2 with props. Lv 3 Rumourcraft — shift a local faction's attitude by 1 step in downtime. Lv 6 Leverage — once per scene, Advantage with real leverage; Dire Failure burns it. Lv 9 Vanish in Motion — Stealth in semi-public after a social action. Lv 12 Knife in the Conversation — at 0 Composure, choose yield or resentful agreement. Lv 15 Network — small contact web; one info question per session. Lv 18 Black Seal — once per arc, collapse a minor power's standing.

Specialities: Deception (Impersonation); Stealth (Crowds); Investigation (Surveillance); Persuasion (Blackmail).

Magical Callings

10) Chirurgeon — Hands and Herb

Healer / field medic / anti-condition support. Main Abilities: Wit; Fortitude; Will; Bearing. Starting: Medicine (Trained), two skills at Trained, one Save at Trained. Comes with a Healer's Satchel.

Lv 1 Field Doctrine — Stabilise / Staunch +2; Great Success also removes Shaken or Tier I Exhaustion. Lv 3 Triage Under Fire — once per round, 1 AP to drop an ally to 1 EP instead of 0. Lv 6 Clean Hands, Clean Wounds — two allies per Short Rest treatment. Lv 9 Pain Management — choose to remove Bleeding, downgrade Exhaustion 1 tier, or remove Overstrained. Lv 12 Surgeon's Authority — first Fortitude Save vs Bleeding/Poison/Disease/Shock/Stagger each scene at Advantage for allies within 2 paces. Lv 15 Recuperative Care — first Short Rest each day +10% EP; Long Rest under your care removes +1 Exhaustion tier. Lv 18 Refuse Death — once per scene, replace a failed Downed save with Partial Success.

Specialities: Medicine (Surgery); Herbalism (Field Remedies); Craft (Splints and Prosthetics); Bearing (Bedside Command).

11) Arcanist (v2) — Mana and Measure

Battlefield caster, ward-maker, counterwork. Main Abilities: Mana; Wit; Will. Starting: Arcana (Trained), Ritual or Investigation (Initiate), two skills at Trained, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Structured Casting — once per round ignore one casting penalty; Great Success on Mana cast regains +2 MP. Lv 3 Ward Reflex — 1 AP Counterwork (opposed Mana/Will) to reduce a spell. Lv 6 Geometer of Battle — control spell Great Success imposes one extra tactical condition. Lv 9 Efficient Channel — first Mana spell each scene -3 MP. Lv 12 Spell-Thread Precision — Focused casts ignore +2 DL/cover. Lv 15 Calm Mind — Will Save downgrades magic-induced Shaken/Frightened. Lv 18 Lattice of Denial — 2-pace Warded zone for one round.

Specialities: Arcana (Counterwork); Ritual (Wards); Lore (Imperial Arcana); Craft (Runes and Devices).

12) Hemomancer — Blood and Hunger

Self-buff striker, emergency healer. Main Abilities: Fortitude; Will; Might (or Mana). Starting: Attunement or Medicine (Trained), Melee or Arcana (Initiate), two skills at Trained, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Crimson Exchange — pay EP instead of MP; first time per scene -2 EP. Lv 3 Coagulate — 1 AP to end Bleeding on self auto, on ally with Medicine roll; Bleeding you inflict requires treatment/magic. Lv 6 Red Frenzy — Wounded grants +1 AP (non-cast) once per round; Grievously Wounded grants +2 damage on first hit instead. Lv 9 Blood Sutures — Short Rest, convert EP into ally healing. Lv 12 Scarred Resilience — Wounded penalty -1; Bleeding 1 EP/turn (unless Void/Divine). Lv 15 Predator's Recovery — dropping a foe regains 1d6 EP. Lv 18 Blood Sovereignty — once per scene, cannot drop below 1 EP for one round.

13) Ordained — Divine and Oath

Protector, miracle support. Main Abilities: Bearing; Will; Mana; Fortitude. Starting: Ritual (Trained), Persuasion or Command (Trained), two skills at Initiate, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Sacred Authority — Sacred Oath with Advantage on a roll directly serving it once per scene. Lv 3 Grace Under Fire — healing does not open you up in melee; Great Success removes Shaken. Lv 6 Consecration — 2 AP, scene-long blessed area. Lv 9 Judgement — once per round, impose Frightened/Shaken on corrupted/undead/profane (Will resists). Lv 12 Shield of Faith — allies within 2 paces +2 vs fear/domination/taint. Lv 15 Unbroken Vow — Will Save downgrades sacred backlash. Lv 18 Miracle of Staying — once per scene, stabilise a dying ally at 0 EP; they awaken at 1 EP after the scene.

14) Soulbinder — Soul and Anchor

Anti-possession, identity warfare, binding, exorcism. Main Abilities: Will; Bearing; Wit. Starting: Attunement (Trained), Ritual (Initiate), two skills at Trained, one Save at Trained.

Lv 1 Anchored Self — once per scene reduce SI loss by 1. Lv 3 Severance — Advantage on ending Dominated / possession; Great Success Disrupts the controller. Specialities: Attunement (Spirits); Ritual (Exorcism); Lore (True Names); Bearing (Dread Presence).

15) Void-Touched — Void and Absence (RESTRICTED)

Negation, scene-breaker, anti-magic terror. Main Abilities: Will; Mana. Starting: Mana Save (Trained), Attunement (Trained), two skills at Initiate. Begins with Corruption 1.

Lv 1 Negation — once per scene declare a magical effect Unmade. Lv 3 Hollow Presence — immune to mundane fear. Lv 6 Unstable Reality — Void Partial Success always introduces a complication. Lv 9 Silence of the Pit — 2 AP, 2-pace zone where Tier 2+ spells fail unless Mana Save. Lv 12 Erasure Touch — Void attacks bypass armour and shields entirely. Lv 15 Marked — Advantage on Intimidation, Disadvantage on normal diplomacy. Lv 18 The End of a Working — once per arc, destroy a magical item / ward / ritual anchor permanently.

Specialities: Attunement (Void Sensing); Intimidation (Dread); Lore (Forbidden); Stealth (Disappear).

Pressure System

Some characters exert weight on a battlefield without striking a blow. The Pressure system models that weight: command, authority, dread, arcane presence. A pressure character does not deal damage so much as cost the enemy actions.

Pressure Value (PV)

PV = Bearing modifier + Social Training tier + Standing (only when authority is recognised).

Tiers: Initiate +1, Trained +3, Skilled +5, Expert +7, Master +9, Legendary +11. Standing applies only against structured enemies (soldiers, guards, citizens, civil servants), not against bandits or beasts.

Radius: 2 paces, or the same room/engagement. Target must perceive you.

Pressure Check (Start of Enemy Turn)

Each affected enemy makes a Will Save against DC = 10 + PV.

  • Success. No effect.
  • Partial Failure (miss by 1-2). Suppressed: -2 to attacks until end of turn.
  • Failure (miss by 5-9). Shaken: -1 to all rolls.
  • Dire Failure (miss by 10+). Frightened: cannot willingly move closer; -2 vs the source.

Pressure is checked once per turn, not stacked repeatedly.

Arcane Pressure (APr)

Magical Callings project a sibling form of pressure. APrV = Mana modifier + Magic Training tier + Corruption (if applicable). Creatures immune to fear may still suffer Suppressed — the body hesitates even when the mind does not.

Calling tweaks: Arcanists may substitute Wit for Corruption; Hemomancers gain +1 APrV while Wounded; Ordained impose Disadvantage on the save of corrupted/undead/profane; Soulbinders impose Disadvantage on possessing spirits; Void-Touched force a minor complication on Partial Success or worse.

Social Actions in Structured Scenes

In tense scenes, AP applies as in combat. Common actions: Press (1 AP, direct argument), Reframe (1 AP, shift topic), Intimidate (2 AP, fear or threat), Charm (2 AP, rapport), Expose (2 AP, reveal contradiction), Withdraw (1 AP, stall).

Rally (1 AP) removes Shaken or ignores one Wounded penalty on an ally's next roll. Hold the Line (2 AP) grants two allies within 2 paces +1 DL until next turn. Command: Advance/Back/Kneel (2 AP) forces a Will Save vs 10+PV; on failure, lose 1 AP or reposition. Demand Surrender

Limit

Each social character may cause only three major Social Effects per scene (Rally, Expose, Command, Demand Surrender). Pressure aura does not count toward this limit.

Inter-Calling Features

Some deeds are shared between Callings. These rules let players cooperate cleanly without "multi-class chaos".

Shared Actions (anyone may use)

Aid (1 AP). Ally's next roll: Assist (+2) or Set Up (success vs DC 15 grants Advantage instead). Only one Aid per roll.
Coordinate (2 AP). You and one ally within 2 paces; Command check vs DC 15; on success both gain +1 AP this round (for Move/Guard/Press/Reframe/Reload only, not extra strikes).
Protect (1 AP, reaction). Impose -2 on the attack on an adjacent ally. If this turns hit → miss, you take half damage.
Covering Action (1 AP). Declare a lane. First enemy through it eats a reaction shot or strike (no multi-strike penalty).

Synergy Actions

Shield & Spear (Legionary + Melee, 2 AP) — Legionary Guards, striker gains +2 to hit; Great Success leaves target Exposed.
Pin & Finish (Ranged + Melee, 2 AP) — Aimed Shot Pins, melee closes and Focused Strikes without overextension penalty.
Break & Storm (Vanguard + ally, 3 AP) — Vanguard's Heavy Blow hits, ally moves and strikes ignoring engagement penalty; Great Success ignores Guard +2 DL.
The Public Trap (Spymaster + Envoy/Magistrate, 2 AP) — Expose then Press; both succeed: target -4 Composure; either fails: target +2 Composure.
Calm & Cut (Envoy/Magistrate + Martial, 2 AP) — diplomat removes Shaken/Panicked; martial's next Strike ignores Wounded penalty.

Cross-Calling Training

At levels 6 and 12, instead of your normal Calling Feature, take one feature from another Calling (Level 1 only, or a Technique Package). You must be Trained (+3) in the relevant area. Borrowed features never scale unless they say so.

Multicalling

You may have a Primary Calling (full progression) and one Secondary. At levels 3, 9, 15, you may take a Secondary feature instead of a Primary one: Secondary Lv1 at 3, Secondary Lv3 at 9, Secondary Lv6 at 15. Secondary caps at Level 6 depth. Hard limit: two Callings, never more.

Magic Systems

Arshmunnu recognises five magical traditions. Each draws on a different cost and is bound by a different etiquette.

Mana Magic — Aether's Obedient Fire

Craft magic. Uses MP = (Mana × 5) + (Will × 3) + (Level × 4). Trainings: Arcana (battle), Ritual (slow), Attunement (sense). Spell tiers cost 3 MP × Tier (Tier 1 = 3 MP, … Tier 5 = 15 MP).

Three modes: Spell Attack (vs DL), Spell Save (vs target Save), Spell Test (vs DC). Excels at elemental shaping, wards, illusions, force, detection. Weak at healing flesh, resurrection, bloodline curses, fate-oaths.

Backlash on Dire Failure or failed Overcast Mana Save: 1d6 EP burn, Overstrained, Stealth ruined, lose 1 AP and Off-Balance, Drained, or wrong attention.

Recovery. Breather (10 min): Mana mod + Will mod, once per hour. Short Rest: 25% Max MP (two per day before diminishing returns). Long Rest: full MP unless Drained.

Concentration: hold one working at a time; Will Save (DC 10 or half damage) when struck or it ends. Sustain: 1 MP per round or per minute outside combat.

Blood Magic (Hemomancy) — Of Life Given and Taken

No MP. Uses Vital Cost (EP): T1 3 EP, T2 6, T3 10, T4 15, T5 21. This damage ignores armour, shields, and resistances. Accepting a Condition (Bleeding, Wounded, Overstrained, Fatigued, Drained) reduces EP cost by half the spell tier (round up).

External blood reduces cost by half and grants +2 to casting — and risks Corruption.

Casting: d20 + Will or Mana + Hemomancy Training. Corruption Save (Will vs DC 12 + Tier) on Dire Failure or repeated misuse.

Healing: heal EP equal to double the EP spent; remove Bleeding or Wounded automatically; often inflicts Fatigued or Overstrained.

Corruption Marks. Each: -1 social with sensitive beings; reduced divine effect; occult detection. 3+: Blood magic +1, Mana -1. 6+: hunted as tainted; Void whispers.

Divine Magic — Authority, Judgement, and Grace

Uses Favour per deity (-3 to +6), not a pool. Favour gates spell access: -3 Forsaken, -1 Strained (T1 only), 0 Neutral (T1-2), +1-2 Accepted (T1-3), +3-4 Favoured (T1-4), +5-6 Chosen (T1-5).

Invocation: 2 AP. Roll d20 + Bearing or Will + Theology/Rite Training. Standard DC = 10 + Tier; opposed: enemy Save vs 10 + Favour + Training.

Bound by Oaths (Minor, Major, Sacred). Breaking — Minor: -1 Favour temp; Major: -2 Favour, atone; Sacred: immediate Forsaken. Atonement: confession, restitution, rite, sacrifice.

Healing: restore Tier × 6 EP; remove Bleeding, Shaken, or Fatigued; requires target consent and good standing. Cannot heal severe Corruption without consequence.

Soul Magic — Essence, Identity, the Price That Does Not Fade

Uses Soul Integrity (SI) = 10 + Will mod + Level. Healthy 7+. Fractured 4-6 (-1 Will saves, echoes). Unmoored 1-3 (Disadvantage on Soul rolls, possession risk). 0: soul collapse.

SI cost by tier: T1 1 SI, T2 2, T3 3, T4 5, T5 7. Cost ignores mitigation and is permanent unless treated. Bound souls (Soul Tokens) substitute, but require a Soul Save (DC 12 + Tier) or lose 1 SI anyway and gain Soul Taint.

Casting: d20 + Will or Bearing + Soulcraft Training. Cannot be spammed; consequences are surgical and lasting. Excels at binding, severing possession, anchoring ghosts, identity work, soul healing. Weak at damage, area, repetition.

Void Magic — Absence, Unmaking, and Hunger

No pool. Costs Corruption, irreducible EP, AP loss, Void Marks, Reality Backlash, or memory. T1 +1 Corruption, T2 +2, T3 +3, T4 +5, T5 +7. Every working adds Corruption automatically — you only choose how fast it grows.

Casting: d20 + Will or Mana + Voidcraft Training. Trainings include Voidcraft, Occult, Eschatology, Forbidden Rite. Excels at dispelling, severing divine connection, unbinding, ending. Weak at creation, healing, precision over time.

Absolute Rule: after any Void working, you may not cast Void magic again until the scene ends, unless you accept automatic Dire Failure. Void magic also draws GM-tracked Attention.
