A Trial of Knowledge upon the World of Arshmunnu

✦ ✦ ✦

Traveller, scholar, or idle reader — should thou seek to measure the depth of thine own learning of the Great and Eternal World, then let this codex be thy mirror. Questions await, drawn from the chronicles, the songs, the magicks, and the bloody ledgers of empire. Should a question confound thee entirely, thou may declare "I Know Not" — the answer shall be revealed, but no mark awarded.
